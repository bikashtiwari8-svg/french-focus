      Settings
      How it decides: the camera pauses the clock if your face is gone, you turn away or look up, your eyes stay closed, or a phone is in view. Looking down at a book is fine. When screen watching is on, every few seconds it reads the text on your screen. Your study websites and pages that are mostly French count as study. YouTube, Netflix, Instagram and similar sites ring the alarm unless what's on screen is French. Pages full of English or other non-French text also ring it, except during "Book work" and "Speaking", where only distracting sites count. In "Speaking" mode you can look around freely; it watches your lips instead and pauses if you stop talking for a few seconds.
